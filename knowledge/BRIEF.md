## The problem

An engineer on site in the US/UK/Germany costs ₹1.5–2 crore a year; the same company can hire us remotely from India, or on a visa, for ₹20–50 lakh and not notice. Recently funded startups exploit that gap; the hyped names do not. We are two final-year students with real shipped AI/infra work and normal (not Codeforces) DSA. ZoNuLy finds those companies and roles, grades each against our resume, finds 10–30 real people per company and labels who can refer, writes one honest email each, stops for our approval, sends 25 a day, tracks replies, follows up once, and puts every "yes" on our calendar. Judgement only matters at "yes, send that to that person". Full statement: system docs/Motive /MOTIV.md.


- **You never see most of the good jobs** — Funded startups post to their own careers page (an ATS) and wait; aggregators see the role after hundreds have applied.
- **You can't tell which ones are worth your time** — "AI Engineer" spans eight-years-of-ML to founding-engineer-for-a-strong-student; only reading the JD against the resume tells you which. Grade decides order and effort, never whether we try.
- **Cold applications go nowhere** — A portal submission is one of 800; a weak referral from an engineer moves you to a stack of ten.
- **Referrals don't scale by hand** — Find a person, their role, their email, their work; write something non-templated; send; follow up; schedule — twenty minutes each, and we have coursework.
- **The pay gap is geographic and the target is funded startups abroad** — US/UK/DE remote-from-India or on-visa at any well-paid local rate; India on-site only at ₹30 LPA+. Not Google/Microsoft/Amazon. Visa feasibility (DE > UK > US) must be scored, not assumed.
- **Who can actually refer** — 10–30 people per company, each classified SDE1/2/3, staff, EM, DevOps, founder/CTO, recruiter/HR. Engineers and EMs first, founders next, recruiters last.
- **A "yes" must not get lost** — Replies that send a Meet link, an assessment or an interview slot go on our calendar with a notification; two companies on one slot get a reschedule draft for approval.
- **Two of us, one pipeline** — Per-candidate resumes, scores, drafts, Gmail accounts and send budgets; shared companies, jobs and contacts; never both of us to the same person in the same month.

## Constraints and guarantees (never cut)

- `constraint:send-cap-25` **25 emails a day, each irreversible** — The real ceiling. A personal Gmail sending 200 lookalike messages gets flagged, and the flagged account is the one interviews arrive at. Every upstream decision is about spending 25 well.
- `constraint:human-review` **Nothing sends without a human reading it** — The review queue is the only path to send. Chosen mode, not a default.
- `constraint:local-only` **(retired) Model runs on the laptop; no LLM API key** — Retired 2026-08-31 by D18. Replaced by — no secret leaves the laptop, every outbound model call is logged, priced and capped. The resume and drafts do go to OpenRouter.
- `constraint:no-local-model` **No Ollama, no local model, ever** — Every attempt to run Ollama locally broke the system. All model calls go through OpenRouter behind a ledger and caps; a CI grep fails on any ollama import.
- `constraint:8gb-ram` **The machine has 8 GB of RAM and no model runtime** — Ollama wrecked the machine every time. With no local model the pipeline is I/O-bound and 8 GB is plenty; the constraint now means no daemons and no local inference of any kind.
- `constraint:free-tier` **Free tiers only for data APIs** — No paid Apollo/SerpAPI/Findymail/MillionVerifier. Revisit only if verified-contact rate drops below ~20%.
- `constraint:no-linkedin-automation` **No logged-in LinkedIn automation, ever** — A banned LinkedIn destroys the exact asset being built — the ability to be referred. LinkedIn is in no scraper module.
- `constraint:single-user-laptop` **One user, one machine, one SQLite file** — No Postgres, no daemons, no cloud scheduler. Everything that exists because of multi-tenancy is out.
- `constraint:ten-minute-review` **Daily review must take ~10 minutes** — If review takes forty minutes the system has failed at its only human step. Unmeasured so far.
- `constraint:context-continuity` **A new session must not start from scratch** — Decisions, gaps and what-changed-when must be queryable, not re-derived from six documents every time. This is what the knowledge graph is for.
- `constraint:student-budget` **₹0 recurring; at most ₹1,000–2,500/month if paid data raises who we reach** — We are students. Every paid call sits behind a daily budget gate and a cache; the baseline must work at zero.
- `constraint:visa-reality` **Visa feasibility is scored, never assumed** — US on-site for a new grad is an H-1B lottery; UK needs a sponsor licence; Germany's Blue Card is feasible with a degree and a €48k+ offer. Remote-from-India is the realistic first path abroad.
- `constraint:one-of-us-per-contact` **Never both of us to the same person in the same month** — Two near-identical emails from two students at one college a week apart reads as spam and burns the company for both.
- `constraint:approval-first` **Nothing happens without permission — in the app and in building it** — Every action has a tier (local read · free external read · spend · write to the world · forbidden). Spend and world-writes need a grant; anything without one creates a request and waits. Building follows the same rule — one proposed, approved step at a time (FINAL-PLAN-V2 §13–14).
- `guarantee:human-gate` **Nothing sends without a human** — sender.send_email() refuses any email whose status isn't approved; approval is only settable from the approve route; no scheduled job calls send. _(enforced by: outreach/, Review queue — edit · approve · reject; the only path to send)_
- `guarantee:no-fabrication` **Nothing is invented** — Never a project, number or shared connection that isn't in the source material. Currently prompt-level only — the mechanical check is GAP-2. _(enforced by: Researcher reads their repos + bio → one honest hook, or null)_
- `guarantee:budget-spent-well` **25/day, irreversibly spent, visible everywhere** — sent_today() counts sent rows since local midnight and gates every send. A count, not yet a ledger — GAP-1. _(enforced by: Daily cap as a count — sent rows since local midnight)_
- `guarantee:confidence-labels` **Every contact carries a confidence tier, never collapsed** — verified | pattern-guessed | scraped, stated on every insert, shown as a badge everywhere, used to order sends. _(enforced by: verified | pattern-guessed | scraped — never defaulted, never collapsed, db.py)_
- `guarantee:one-followup` **At most one follow-up per thread** — Email.followup_sent is set when the follow-up is queued, not sent, so a second can't be created even if the first is never approved. _(enforced by: Exactly one follow-up after 5 silent days, back through the review queue)_
- `guarantee:data-stays-local` **No secret leaves the laptop; every outbound call is logged, priced and capped** — Revised 2026-08-31 (D18). The resume and drafts do go to OpenRouter; keys stay in the environment; only the four network adapters may call out; every paid call is a ledger row under a cap you set. _(enforced by: OpenRouter-only model layer — aliases cheap / judge / writer, num_retries 0, think-stripping, a cost row per call, budget gate, no key → stage does not run, agent_run + agent_run_cost + apify_run_log — every invocation a row with phases, status, tokens, INR, error, parent_run_id, CI greps and tests — no ollama import, no network outside the four adapters, no untimed wait, Apify Bearer only, no AUTO_REJECT, EXECUTE_OUTREACH unheld, every tool has a capability, every constant pinned)_
- `guarantee:no-auto-apply` **No auto-apply, no contact-list building** — There is no application-submission code path and no export path. One person, one role, one ask.

## Settled decisions

- **D1 — Python, not TypeScript** (`decision:d1-python`) — The value of the system sits in the scraping, PDF and email ecosystem, which is Python's. The frontend stays Next.js over REST. Consequence — the portal's layering lint doesn't port; the rule holds by convention.
  - adopts: Python pipeline + Next.js UI over REST
  - rejects: Next.js + Drizzle + Node worker for everything
- **D2 — (reversed by D18) qwen3:4b, the size the machine can actually hold** (`decision:d2-model-size`) — REVERSED 2026-08-31 — Ollama wrecks the machine; see D18. Every draft assumed 14B and 16 GB; the machine has 8. 4B ranks correctly on real postings (verified by hand); calibration against real screens is still unmeasured. Fixed num_ctx + keep_alive 30m are load-bearing.
  - adopts: qwen3:4b + nomic-embed-text, resident together
  - rejects: qwen2.5:14b scoring + small model tiering
- **D3 — (reversed by D18) No hosted model; local wins** (`decision:d3-no-hosted-model`) — REVERSED 2026-08-31 — OpenRouter is the only provider; see D18. TOOLS budgeted Claude for drafts; ORCHESTRATION said "there is no LLM API key, and that is the point." Local wins — it was the original constraint, the review queue catches weak prose, and llm.py is a one-line seam. Pull that seam first if reply rates are bad.
  - adopts: Drafts written by the local model — 90–130 words, plain text; One thin llm.py — the only place a model is called
  - rejects: Claude Sonnet for drafts (local for everything else)
- **D4 — The orchestrator is code; there are no supervisor agents** (`decision:d4-deterministic-orchestrator`) — Every supervisor decision is a ten-line policy with a config value. What survived — the state machine, narrow workers, typed contracts, depth cap, fault isolation. What was dropped — envelopes, capability narrowing, the run tree.
  - adopts: Deterministic orchestrator — APScheduler + per-item commits, no LLM supervisors; Typed contracts between stages (RawJob, Salary, Score); One dead source never kills a run
  - rejects: 5 LLM supervisors over 18 workers with typed envelopes; Per-source circuit breaker with a source_health table; Append-only artifacts with content_hash; everything else re-derivable
- **D5 — The GitHub email spike came back good, bimodally** (`decision:d5-github-spike`) — Supabase 12/12, Affirm 20/20, GitLab 5/5, Observe.AI 0 (no org). Person-event mining yields nothing (GitHub removed commit detail from PushEvent); the repo commits endpoint still carries author emails. Infra/devtools companies are cheap to contact; companies without public code should be skipped, not guessed at.
  - adopts: GitHub repo-commits endpoint → commit.author.email; No public code and no team page → skip the company, don't guess
- **D6 — Free tier only; no paid finders or verification credits** (`decision:d6-free-tier`) — The waterfall can take paid tiers but free tiers are doing the work. Hunter's 25/mo is reserved for learning a company's pattern. Revisit if verified rate drops below ~20%.
  - adopts: Hunter.io free tier (25/mo) spent on patterns, not people; Learn the company's email pattern, apply it to every other name, MX-check
  - rejects: Findymail / Anymailfinder + MillionVerifier credits
- **D7 — SMTP RCPT probing is off; TOOLS.md was right** (`decision:d7-smtp-off`) — Built on by default, which was wrong. Port 25 is blocked residentially, big providers accept every recipient at the edge, and probing risks an IP listing. MX lookup stays.
  - adopts: MX lookup always; SMTP RCPT probe off
  - rejects: SMTP RCPT verification probe
- **D8 — Three confidence tiers, never collapsed** (`decision:d8-three-tiers`) — verified / pattern-guessed / scraped, named for what the user needs to distinguish. Shown everywhere, never defaulted.
  - adopts: verified | pattern-guessed | scraped — never defaulted, never collapsed
- **D9 — SQLite, one file, no vector extension** (`decision:d9-no-sqlite-vec`) — Plain-Python cosine over cached vectors is sub-second at 1,100 postings. Revisit past ~50k.
  - adopts: SQLite, one file, no daemon; Resume vector cached in a Setting row, invalidated by content hash
  - rejects: sqlite-vec extension for embeddings
- **D10 — The knowledge graph lives in SQLite with NetworkX on top, not in a graph database** (`decision:d10-kg-in-sqlite`) — Same logic as D9 — Neo4j needs a daemon, Kùzu was archived in late 2025, LLM-memory frameworks want a server-class backend. Two tables and an FTS5 index in jobhunter.db keep it in the one file that's backed up; NetworkX gives centrality, paths and GraphML export in memory.
  - adopts: Knowledge graph — data + context in one queryable store
- **D11 — SQLite stays; CSV is an interface, not the store** (`decision:d11-sqlite-not-csv`) — The restated problem asked for CSV and no database. A CSV cannot hold dedup keys, scores, contacted-when, budgets or reply state. Export and import CSVs exist for the two of us; the state stays in jobhunter.db.
  - adopts: CSV export of companies / contacts / sends / events, CSV import of companies through the audit gate
  - rejects: Store companies in a CSV instead of a database
- **D12 — LinkedIn data comes from public no-cookies channels or not at all** (`decision:d12-linkedin-no-cookies-only`) — Free path first (GitHub commits, site, pattern). Where that yields nothing at a graded company, an Apify no-cookies employee harvest at ~$0.15/company behind a $0.50/day gate. Our own LinkedIn is used by hand for the alumni filter and never automated.
  - adopts: Apify no-cookies LinkedIn employee harvest, budget-gated at $0.50/day, cached 24h; Apify response cache keyed on (actor, version, input hash) with TTL, plus a daily cost ledger
- **D13 — "Multi-agent" means narrow LLM workers under a deterministic conductor** (`decision:d13-agents-are-workers`) — Salary extractor, fit judge, role classifier, researcher, drafter, reply classifier, schedule extractor, follow-up drafter. Each has a typed input and output and a budget; none calls another; none can send; deterministic code decides what runs. D4 restated with a roster.
  - adopts: Role classifier — engineer | senior_engineer | manager | founder | recruiter | other, plus seniority; Schedule extractor — a positive reply becomes {intent, proposed_times, link, deadline, needs_action}; Deterministic orchestrator — APScheduler + per-item commits, no LLM supervisors
- **D14 — Target regions and pay are config; the scorer knows about visa feasibility** (`decision:d14-regions-and-feasibility`) — search.regions with per-region floors; a fifth rubric dimension for remote-from-India / sponsorship / new-grad visa realism; a hard cap on US on-site with no sponsorship statement.
  - adopts: Target regions with per-region pay floors — US/UK/DE any well-paid, India ≥ ₹30 LPA on-site; Fifth rubric dimension — does this posting allow remote-from-India, sponsor, or sit where a new-grad visa is realistic
- **D15 — Two candidates, one pipeline, one of us per contact per month** (`decision:d15-two-candidates`) — Per-candidate scores, drafts, sends, budgets and Gmail; shared companies, jobs and contacts; the cooldown is enforced in the drafter.
  - adopts: Two candidates, one pipeline — candidate table, per-candidate scores, drafts, Gmail and budgets; A contact is written to by at most one of us per 30 days; one of us per company per role
- **D16 — OpenRouter for the writing tasks, local for everything else, both behind the permission gate** (`decision:d16-openrouter-writing-tier`) — Revises D3. Drafts, follow-ups and the approval assistant may run on a cheap hosted model via one OpenRouter key (≤ 80 calls/day, ≤ ₹400/month); scoring, embeddings, research and reply classification stay local; any failure or a missing key falls back to local. Disabled until enabled in permissions.yaml.
  - adopts: Per-task routing in llm.py — draft / followup / review to OpenRouter, score / reply / research local, local fallback always; Approval assistant — a review worker that flags each draft for fabrication, tone, length and the one-ask rule; never approves
  - rejects: Claude Sonnet for drafts (local for everything else)
- **D17 — Nothing runs or gets built without permission** (`decision:d17-approval-first`) — The permission gate is build step 0 and every later step is gated by it; the build itself proceeds one approved step at a time with a note after each.
  - adopts: Permission gate — tiers 0–4, permissions.yaml grants, approval_request + action_log, pending/grant/deny CLI and dashboard tab; action_log — every tier 1–3 call with cost, candidate and outcome; Build protocol — every step proposed with scope, files, tools, cost, verification and rollback; approved by number; one at a time; kg note after each
- **D18 — No Ollama, no local model; OpenRouter is the only model provider** (`decision:d18-no-local-model`) — Reverses D2 and D3. Ollama has wrecked the machine every time. Aliases cheap / judge / writer over one key, num_retries 0, a cost row per call, daily and monthly caps in permissions.yaml, the lexical fit gate at the wide end of the funnel so the model only reads plausible jobs.
  - adopts: OpenRouter-only model layer — aliases cheap / judge / writer, num_retries 0, think-stripping, a cost row per call, budget gate, no key → stage does not run; Model-free lexical fit — rarity-weighted vocabulary overlap between resume and JD, stemmed, stop-listed; Triage agent on the cheap alias — worth scoring? region? remote? sponsorship stated?
  - rejects: qwen3:4b + nomic-embed-text, resident together; Drafts written by the local model — 90–130 words, plain text; Embedding prefilter by percentile, not threshold; Resume vector cached in a Setting row, invalidated by content hash
- **D19 — Agents are pydantic-ai workers that can only propose; capabilities and budgets are enforced by the runtime, not the prompt** (`decision:d19-agents-propose-runtime-enforces`) — gtm-arch's agent runtime and proposal path, MULTI-AGENT's depth cap and envelopes, without Temporal. Every write is a fact_proposal or draft_proposal; every run is a ledger row; EXECUTE_OUTREACH is held by nobody.
  - adopts: pydantic-ai agent runtime — typed I/O, narrow tool surface, enforce_capability first line of every tool, budget triple, EXECUTE_OUTREACH held by nobody; fact_proposal + draft_proposal — every agent write is a proposal; facts promote at ≥ 0.85, review 0.70–0.85, review-with-reason below; drafts always HITL; never auto-reject; agent_run + agent_run_cost + apify_run_log — every invocation a row with phases, status, tokens, INR, error, parent_run_id; budgets.py — every timeout, threshold and cap in one file, pinned by a test; no AUTO_REJECT symbol; artifact table — every fetched document append-only with content_hash; evidence spans point here; Faithfulness gate (span exists · span supports) + publish gate (every proper noun and number matches a span or profile field) — strip, never rewrite
  - rejects: 5 LLM supervisors over 18 workers with typed envelopes
- **D20 — Three scraping channels behind one politeness core, one staging area, one audit gate, driven by a launchd tick** (`decision:d20-three-channel-scraping`) — CHOKEPOINTS' engine shape — keyless channel at the documented rate 24/7, Apify no-cookies channel behind a budget, Brave research channel returning only cited facts — replaces per-scraper clients and APScheduler.
  - adopts: fetch.py — the only place the network is touched for free sources; robots when real, per-host rate, disk cache by URL hash, real UA, 20 s / 2 MB bounds; CI grep for any other httpx call; Every channel writes staging; promote previews by default, --apply refuses on a failing audit and restores the backup; tick.py under launchd — hourly, fcntl-locked, spends this hour's rate budget and today's money budget, most-stale work first, source_health with backoff, exits in minutes; Brave Search + politeness-core fetch + cheap model → observations and facts that each carry an artifact span; uncited items dropped; source quality classified separately; apify.py — PINNED_ACTORS in ~ form with version, output_model and estimated cost; run-sync endpoint; 30-day cache keyed on (actor, version, input hash); budget pre-flight; ?maxCharge; shape mismatch non-retryable; Bearer only; Daily report — sources at 0, scores, people by channel, drafts owed, sends left, replies by class, events today, money spent by vendor and alias against caps; Every multi-item stage appends and fsyncs per item and skips ids already present on restart — a relaunch never re-spends a paid call
  - rejects: Per-source circuit breaker with a source_health table

## Failure modes, most likely first

- **A source breaks silently** — Board changes shape, scraper returns 0, nothing errors. Signal — one source's count drops to 0 while others are normal. _(mitigated by: One dead source never kills a run, A source returning 0 twice running is reported as a failure)_
- **The human stops reviewing** — Queue depth grows, approvals go to zero. A backlog of unread drafts is pure waste. _(mitigated by: Drafts expire after 7 days, Keyboard-driven review (j k a r), inline edit)_
- **The local model degrades on long runs** — Ollama evicting/reloading under 8 GB pressure — a scoring pass suddenly takes minutes per job. Fixed by fixed num_ctx + keep_alive; re-check if a third model is ever added. _(mitigated by: qwen3:4b + nomic-embed-text, resident together, One thin llm.py — the only place a model is called)_
- **Bounces damage the sending account** — Guessed addresses fail; Gmail notices the pattern. _(mitigated by: At most 5 of the 25 daily sends to pattern-guessed addresses, verified | pattern-guessed | scraped — never defaulted, never collapsed, Verified-first ordering, then role relevance)_
- **Drafts read as templates** — Reply rate flat and low. If you find yourself approving without editing, that's the signal. _(mitigated by: Review queue — edit · approve · reject; the only path to send, Voice check against the corpus of previously approved drafts, Warmth ladder — alumni > shared OSS > same city > same stack > content > cold > recruiter)_
- **GitHub rate limit stalls contact discovery** — 60/hr unauthenticated stalls after ~3 companies. _(mitigated by: GitHub personal access token — 60/hr → 5,000/hr)_
- **Commit mining yields nothing for companies without public code** — Expected, not a bug. Site scrape is the fallback; if both fail, skip rather than guess. _(mitigated by: Company team/about/careers page scrape, No public code and no team page → skip the company, don't guess)_

## Still open

- **Does 4B stay good enough as volume grows?** — Ranking is right today. If reply rates are poor and drafts read stiff — better local model on a bigger machine first, then hosted model for drafting only.
- **Does review actually take ten minutes?** — Unmeasured — there has been one draft. Measure before scaling to 25/day.
- **Warmth or score as the primary sort?** — TOOLS argues warmth. Untested because warmth isn't built.
- **Is the 70th-percentile prefilter the right number?** — Keeps the top 30% (~330 of 1,100). Chosen from the similarity distribution, not from outcomes.
- **What happens when a company replies from a different address?** — Thread matching by gmail_thread_id handles most of it; person-level identity across addresses is unsolved.
- **Is $0.15/company of Apify data worth more than the same evenings on warmth tiers?** — Measure the verified-email rate of Apify-sourced contacts after pattern inference before deciding.
- **Should low-grade (< 40) roles ever be auto-drafted?** — The plan says no, MOTIV says "still send". Resolve after a month of outcomes.
