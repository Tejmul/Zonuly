ZoNuLy — five marks

Every one is a solid form first and a drawing second, so it survives a 16px tab. All use a single colour, so the same file is black on white and white on black. Each row shows the mark at 96 / 32 / 16px, in a browser tab, and locked up with the wordmark — in both themes.

A · Threshold live on the site

The route through the one

A solid tile — the same square the hero's crowd is made of — with the referral's route cut straight through it as a Z. The route ends in a person: the ring is where it lands. Negative space, so it is one shape at any size, and the strongest thing a tab can hold.

B · Reticle

The instrument on the crowd

The hero's own viewfinder brackets, closed around four people — three hollow, one solid. The most literal of the five: the product is a lens that finds one person. Reads as a camera-focus mark from a distance, which is both its strength and its risk.

C · Seven

A Z made of people

Seven points in the shape of a Z — three, one, three — and the seventh is a target ring: the one we were looking for. Dot-matrix marks are rare and own a lot of character; the diagonal is implied by the centre point, which is what makes it interesting rather than obvious.

D · Door

Walks your resume in

The headline, literally: the Z's last stroke becomes an arrow, and it is walking through an open door. The most story-driven of the five and the one with the most personality; it is also the busiest at 16px, where the door and the arrow start to touch.

E · Bar

The heavy Z, ending in a tile

A fat, square-cut Z — display-weight, like the headline face — whose final stroke is replaced by the one solid tile. The boldest silhouette of the set and the most "brand" at big sizes; at 16px the tile and the bar nearly merge into a plain Z, which may be exactly right.
